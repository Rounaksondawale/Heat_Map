ISRO problem statement · urban heat

Finding the hottest blocks in 50 Indian cities, from space

A U-Net reads Landsat and land-cover layers and predicts which parts of a city run hotter or cooler than the city's own average (late-morning pass, March to May). It learned from 40 cities and was scored on 10 held out from training.
