Built for an ISRO problem statement

ISRO · problem statement

Optimizing urban heat mitigation and cooling strategies via AI/ML

1Heat stress mapsRanked Gi* hotspot clusters
2Drivers of urban heatSix feature groups, split in °C
3Validated AI/ML modelU-Net, R² 0.72 on 10 held-out cities
4Cooling scenariosTrees and cool roofs, re-predicted
5Optimal interventionDose and °C cut per hotspot, e.g. 40 % trees
